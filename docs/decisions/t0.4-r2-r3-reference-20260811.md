## Coverage, missingness, and labels

- 500 users exist in truth; 482 have observed histories/dense exports, so 18
  users are explicitly missing. Cutoff exports contain 1,433 rows. The matched
  three-cutoff comparison uses 475 shared users (380 probe-train, 95 probe-test).
- Dense evaluation has 22,325 labeled rows from 482 users, 6,218 of 7,000
  episodes have a dense row, and 6 of 10 boundary-relative time bins are
  populated. Temporal/routine label coverage is 1.0 on dense rows; routine
  class counts are 14,089 repeated-routine and 1,622 one-off rows. Quadratic
  retrieval/probe/collapse diagnostics use the same deterministic 1,024-row
  subset for both representations and report eligible and sampled counts.
- Frozen future-event known-label coverage is 1.000 for service, action,
  category, and region; 0.778 for geohash-5; and only 0.033 for geohash-7.
  Learned-minus-baseline frozen-probe accuracy deltas are respectively -0.211,
  -0.111, -0.100, +0.044, +0.057, and 0.000. The near-zero geohash-7 coverage
  prevents an affirmative fine-local claim.
- Learned next-event diagnostics separately report train-fitted naive controls.
  Known-label coverage is 1.000 for service/action/category/region, 0.999 for
  geohash-5, and 0.687 for geohash-7; all aggregate values are finite. These
  predictive diagnostics are not representation-quality or disentanglement
  evidence.

## Unsupported R1--R8 claims

- **R1:** unsupported for persistent/routine/context disentanglement. Negative
  persistent/preference probes and a single vector preclude this claim.
- **R2:** partial synthetic spatial diagnostics only; real spatial calibration,
  unseen-POI transfer, and robust fine-local fidelity are unsupported.
- **R3:** observational hour/day/duration probes are available, but controlled
  schedule-shift generalization and explicit routine state are unsupported.
- **R4:** episode response/coherence is measurable, but factorized episode
  context, matched changes, and causal episode adaptation are unsupported.
- **R5:** nuisance-adjusted observational probes are not matched opportunity or
  exposure counterfactuals; preference/opportunity separation is unsupported.
- **R6:** leave-one-service-out sensitivity is measurable, but semantic
  cross-service alignment and transfer are unsupported.
- **R7:** deterministic simulator-view sensitivity is measurable; causal
  invariance and calibration to real GPS, timestamp, and missingness processes
  are unsupported.
- **R8:** held-out-region/unseen-geohash slices are partial and sparse;
  unseen-POI, controlled temporal shift, and external geographic
  generalization are unsupported.

The evidence therefore rejects choosing an aggregate winner. It supports using
the learned model as a responsive, less rank-collapsed diagnostic baseline, not
as a successful persistent-preference or factorized representation.
