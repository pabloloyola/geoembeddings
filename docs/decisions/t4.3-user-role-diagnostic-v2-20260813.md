# T4.3 user-role diagnostic v2 lineage

## Scope

This immutable, simulator-scoped R12 diagnostic lineage uses 200 users, simulator
seed `20260813`, and `configs/embedding/user_role_diagnostic_v2.yaml`. It compares
a statistical baseline with the learned single-vector diagnostic control. Both
retain `selection_role: diagnostic_control`; there is no selected candidate and
the selection-dependent privacy conclusion is unavailable.

The authoritative machine-readable record is
`docs/artifacts/t4.3-user-role-diagnostic-v2-20260813.json`. It indexes every
command, resolved configuration, observed-source and preparation identity,
user-role and participation hash, checkpoint, export, utility report, split,
privacy output, coverage item, and exclusion used here.

## Participation authentication

The learned checkpoint's authenticated `training_participation.json` reports 92
users contributing 1,377 eligible target-training windows and 42 users
contributing 179 validation/checkpoint-selection windows. The frozen role
protocol assigned 103 target-train, 48 target-validation, and 49 target-test
users. Ninety-four target-train users participated in train-fitted preprocessing.

The 49 target-test users are clean whole-pipeline nonparticipants: none fit
preprocessing, contributed target-fitting windows, or contributed checkpoint-
selection windows. They are reported separately from preprocessing participants.

## Frozen support gate

Common support fails for an exact structural population constraint, not a tuned
threshold. Both canonical exports intentionally contain only the 49 target-test
users at the three frozen cutoffs. Thus the exact common export population has
0 target-training members and 49 clean nonmembers before matching. It cannot
meet the predeclared minimum total of 100, minimum per class of 20, or minimum
per stratum of 10. The matched population is empty and no membership attack was
run. No threshold was relaxed post hoc.

## Sensitive probes and conclusion

Sensitive-attribute evaluation ran independently of the membership gate. The
frozen configuration's `age_group` and `household_type` targets were excluded as
public/observed rather than protected leakage targets, while
`leisure_preference_group` was unavailable because it is not in the evaluator's
code-versioned protected-attribute allowlist. Consequently no sensitive attack
metric or bootstrap interval was estimable; these exclusions are preserved in
the immutable reports rather than replaced with another attribute.

This is an unavailable diagnostic result, not evidence of privacy. It neither
relaxes the T2.7 do-not-advance decision nor provides a privacy guarantee.
